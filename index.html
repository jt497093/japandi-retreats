A Luxury Silent Retreat

Step away from the noise of daily life and experience three days of silence in nature.

This intimate retreat takes place at Japandi Lounge, a serene countryside residence designed around calm, simplicity and natural materials.

Unlike most retreats, this experience is intentionally small and comfortable. Only 8 guests participate in each retreat.

A True Digital Detox

For the full duration of the retreat all participants disconnect from phones and digital devices.

This digital detox allows your mind to slow down and your attention to return to the present moment.

Many guests find that just three days without screens dramatically improves clarity, focus and sleep.

The Experience

During the retreat you are guided through a calm rhythm of practices designed to support stillness and reflection.
