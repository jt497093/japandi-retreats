5 Day Silent Retreat

What's it about?

A silent retreat gives you the rare opportunity to step away from the noise, demands and constant stimulation of everyday life.

By removing familiar distractions and everyday conversation, the retreat creates space to slow down, reflect and become more aware of your thoughts, emotions and habits.

The five-day format allows more time for silence, meditation, reflection and guided activities without the usual social distractions. The aim is to return to everyday life with greater clarity and a clearer sense of what matters.
