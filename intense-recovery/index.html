Intensive Recovery Retreat

What's it about?

The Intensive Recovery Retreat is a small-group experience designed to help you step away from screens, routines and constant stimulation. It creates the conditions to slow down, reset physically and mentally, and reconnect with yourself.

The programme combines active challenges with periods of calm. Breathwork, cold exposure, movement, meditation, sound, recovery practices, shared meals and time in nature are brought together in one carefully paced experience.

Nothing is about performing or competing. The aim is to challenge yourself where it feels useful, then create enough space afterwards for the body and mind to settle, recover and process the experience.

Activities are organised and led by experienced professionals who provide clear guidance in a supportive, relaxed environment.

Duration 3 nights / 4 days
Group size Maximum 10 participants
Location Japandi Lounge, Noord-Brabant
Experience required None

Your hosts

Meet the people guiding the sessions and creating the environment for the retreat.

Mike Mol

Musician | Movement & Recovery Therapist

Mike combines his passion for music with expertise in breathwork, cold training, movement and recovery therapy. He leads workshops and expeditions in extraordinary locations, helping people relax, expand their limits and reconnect with themselves.

His work has included collaboration with Wim Hof and breath expert James Nestor, alongside extensive experience in functional breathing, cold exposure, movement, recovery therapy and live music.

Leading: Breathwork, cold exposure, movement, recovery and meditative live music
mikemol.com

Josje

Musician | Therapist & Mindfulness Trainer

Josje is a versatile musician with many years of experience guiding plant medicine ceremonies, ceremonial concerts, and sound healing sessions.

Drawing on her background as a therapist and mindfulness trainer, combined with her intuitive approach as a musician, she creates a safe and supportive space where you are invited to slow down, feel, and become fully present.

Through her voice, music, and the deeply resonant sounds of her sound healing instruments, she invites you to move out of your head and reconnect with your body and heart. The vibrations and sounds can support you in relaxing, opening up, and connecting more deeply with what is alive within you.

Josje intuitively follows what arises in the moment, adapting her guidance to the energy of the group and the ceremony. In this way, she creates a space where there is nothing you need to do or achieve, but where you can simply be, feel, and receive.

Leading: Sound healing, ceremonial music, mindfulness and plant medicine ceremonies

Accommodation

Stay at Japandi Lounge

The Intensive Recovery Retreat takes place at Japandi Lounge, a restored farmhouse in the Noord-Brabant countryside near Den Bosch. The house provides comfortable shared accommodation, spacious communal areas and wellness facilities designed to give the group room to slow down, recover and connect.
