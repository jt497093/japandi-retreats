Intensive Recovery Retreat

What's it about?

The Intensive Recovery Retreat is a small-group experience designed to help you step away from screens, routines and constant stimulation. It creates the conditions to slow down, reset physically and mentally, and reconnect with yourself.

The programme combines active challenges with periods of calm. Breathwork, cold exposure, movement, meditation, sound, recovery practices, shared meals and time in nature are brought together in one carefully paced experience.

Nothing is about performing or competing. The aim is to challenge yourself where it feels useful, then create enough space afterwards for the body and mind to settle, recover and process the experience.

Activities are organised and led by experienced professionals who provide clear guidance in a supportive, relaxed environment.

Duration 3 nights / 4 days
Group size Maximum 10 participants
Location Japandi Lounge, Noord-Brabant
Experience required None

Programme

Below is an example of how a weekend can look. The final programme may vary slightly depending on the group, the weather and the activities chosen.

Day Time Activity
Friday 15:30 Arrival, settle in, explore the house and unwind.
18:00 Welcome dinner, meet the team, connect and enjoy the evening together.
21:00 Opening ceremony with Mike & Josje, live music, blue lotus and gentle breathwork. A relaxed introduction focused on deep rest and sleep: feeling first, then understanding.
Saturday 08:00 Fresh start with stretching, activation and practical breathing. Learn simple techniques to improve everyday breathing and make each breath more effective.
09:00 Introductions, icebreaker and intentions. Meet the group and reflect on what you would like to take from the weekend.
09:30 Breakfast.
10:30 Breathing theory and Q&A. Why the exercises work, what you may feel and time for questions.
11:00 Guided ice-bath session. Preparation, entering the water, warming yourself from within and the benefits of cold exposure. Sessions are individual or in pairs.
12:00 Movement game to warm up further and deepen your connection with nature.
12:30 Lunch followed by free time.
15:30 External activity, for example a canoe trip.
19:00 Dinner, pizza making, sunset and campfire.
Sunday 08:00 Cacao ceremony.
08:30 Deep breathwork session with live music, followed by space to share afterwards.
10:00 Breakfast.
11:00 Nature walk and guided connection exercise. In pairs, one person is blindfolded and guided by the other. Barefoot walking or nearby natural water may be included where suitable.
12:30 Lunch and siesta.
15:30 Hot & Cold recovery session. Alternate between cold immersion and the sauna or hot tub. A larger ice-filled jacuzzi can be used for group sessions.
18:00 Dinner.
20:00 Q&A and sharing circle. How to build the practices into daily life, optionally followed by a sharing circle around the campfire.
Monday 08:00 Final breathwork session, connection and closing.
09:00 Breakfast.
10:00 Final session, reflection and time to integrate the weekend.
12:30 Retreat ends and departure.

His work has included collaboration with Wim Hof and breath expert James Nestor, alongside extensive experience in functional breathing, cold exposure, movement, recovery therapy and live music.

Stay at Japandi Lounge

The Intensive Recovery Retreat takes place at Japandi Lounge, a restored farmhouse in the Noord-Brabant countryside near Den Bosch. The house provides comfortable shared accommodation, spacious communal areas and wellness facilities designed to give the group room to slow down, recover and connect.
