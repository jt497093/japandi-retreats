Intensive Recovery Retreat

What's it about?

The Intensive Recovery Retreat is a small-group experience designed to help you step away from screens, routines and constant stimulation. It creates the conditions to slow down, reset physically and mentally, and reconnect with yourself.

The programme combines active challenges with periods of calm. Breathwork, cold exposure, movement, meditation, sound, recovery practices, shared meals and time in nature are brought together in one carefully paced experience.

Nothing is about performing or competing. The aim is to challenge yourself where it feels useful, then create enough space afterwards for the body and mind to settle, recover and process the experience.

Activities are organised and led by experienced professionals who provide clear guidance in a supportive, relaxed environment.

Duration 3 nights / 4 days
Group size Maximum 10 participants
Location Japandi Lounge, Noord-Brabant
Experience required None

Programme

A balance of guided sessions, shared meals, recovery and unstructured time. The exact programme can be adjusted to the group and conditions.

Day Time Activity
Friday 16:00 Arrival, settle in, explore the house and slow down.
18:00 Welcome dinner, introductions and connection with the group.
21:00 Opening ceremony with live music, sound healing and gentle breathwork for relaxation and sleep.
Saturday 08:00 Morning movement, stretching and practical breathing techniques.
09:00 Group introduction, intentions and what you want to take from the weekend.
09:30 Breakfast.
10:30 Breathing theory, practical explanation and preparation for cold exposure.
11:00 Guided ice-bath session with preparation, breathing and recovery afterwards.
Afternoon Lunch, recovery time and guided activities. Final timing and sessions will be published with the retreat.
Sunday Morning Movement, meditation, breathwork and shared breakfast.
Daytime Recovery workshops, reflection, nature, shared meals and guided experiences.
Monday Morning Final session, reflection, breakfast and time to integrate the weekend.
12:00 Retreat ends and departure.

Programme timings may change slightly depending on weather, group needs and the final combination of facilitators and sessions.
